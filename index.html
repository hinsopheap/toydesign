🔊 Conversation & observations ● OBSERVING — mic and camera active

Talk mode: tap 🎤 and speak. Observe mode: tap 👁 — Atlas listens to everyone, watches through the camera, answers when you say its name (“Atlas …”), and gives advice when you tap 💡.

⚙️ Cloud AI brain (OpenRouter API key) — required for vision and smart advice

Without a key, Atlas uses its built-in offline brain (greetings + math, both languages) and can only log what it hears. With a key (free at openrouter.ai), Atlas understands open conversation, describes what the camera sees, and gives real advice. The key is stored only in this browser; images and transcripts are sent only to OpenRouter.
