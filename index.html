Atlas is a companion: tap a game button, pat its head, or talk to it. Voice works too — say “Atlas, dance!” or «អាត់ឡាស រាំ!». In 👁 Observe mode it listens to everyone, watches the room, and advises you when you tap 💡.

⚙️ Cloud AI brain (OpenRouter API key) — required for vision and smart advice

Without a key, Atlas plays, moves, jokes, sings, does math and greetings in both languages, and logs what it hears. With a key (free at openrouter.ai), Atlas also understands open conversation, describes what the camera sees, and gives real advice. The key is stored only in this browser; images and transcripts go only to OpenRouter.
